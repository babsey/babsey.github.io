I'm Sebastian Spreizer, based in Freiburg, Germany. My work sits at the intersection of
computational neuroscience and research software engineering.

I develop [NEST Desktop](https://github.com/nest-desktop/nest-desktop), a web-based graphical
interface for the [NEST Simulator](https://www.nest-simulator.org/). It lets students and
researchers build spiking neural network models visually, run simulations and analyse the
results directly in the browser.

## Academic background

In 2021 I finished my PhD in computational neuroscience at the University of Freiburg. My research
questions were:

- What role do network connectivity and network dynamics play in the transfer of information
  from one network to another?
- How do neurons interact with network structure to shape network activity dynamics?
- How can the activity dynamics of a network and its information transfer be controlled by
  external stimulation?
