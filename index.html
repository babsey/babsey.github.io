About

Background

Computational neuroscientist and open-source software developer.

I am a passionate code writer for open source software, specializing in the development of web applications for computational neuroscience.

Currently, I am lead developer of NEST Desktop, an educational web application for the NEST Simulation Tool. I also enjoy analyzing and visualizing data from numerical simulations of brain activity.

In 2021 I finished my PhD in computational neuroscience at the University of Freiburg, where I studied how network connectivity and dynamics shape information transfer in neural networks.
